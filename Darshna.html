Darshna is an associate practicing in the areas of civil litigation and family law. She was enrolled to practice before the courts of Pakistan in 2018, and has since gained experience representing clients in property disputes, contractual matters, and civil litigation before the courts of Sindh.

Darshna graduated from the University of Karachi's LLB program in 2017, where she was active in the university's moot court society. Prior to joining Mukesh Kumar Khatri & Co, she gained experience working with a boutique litigation practice in Karachi.

Darshna helps clients feel confident and informed throughout their case by maintaining clear, consistent communication at every stage. She combines careful research and thoughtful negotiation to ensure every client receives personalized, timely representation.

Darshna is based in Karachi, where she grew up and continues to live today. Outside of her practice, she enjoys reading, spending time with family, and exploring the city's food scene.
