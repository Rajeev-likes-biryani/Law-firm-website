Mushtaq is a consultant practicing in the areas of taxation and corporate advisory. He was enrolled to practice before the courts of Pakistan in 2014, and has since built extensive experience advising individuals and businesses on tax disputes, compliance matters, and corporate structuring.

Mushtaq graduated from the University of Punjab's LLB program in 2013, later completing additional certification in taxation law. Prior to joining Mukesh Kumar Khatri & Co, he worked with a tax advisory firm in Karachi, handling matters before the Federal Board of Revenue.

Mushtaq takes a practical, detail-oriented approach to every case, helping clients navigate complex tax matters with clarity and confidence. He works closely with each client to identify solutions that protect their financial interests while ensuring full compliance.

Mushtaq is based in Karachi, where he has lived for most of his life. Outside of his practice, he enjoys chess, reading about economic policy, and spending time with his family.
