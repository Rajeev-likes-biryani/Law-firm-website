Mukesh Kumar Khatri is an Advocate of the Supreme Court of Pakistan, practicing in taxation, civil, criminal, and constitutional law. Over the course of his career, he has represented clients across a wide range of matters, building a reputation for thorough preparation and dedicated advocacy both in and out of the courtroom.

Mukesh completed his law degree and was enrolled to practice before the courts of Pakistan, where he began building the foundation for what would become Mukesh Kumar Khatri & Co. His early years in practice shaped his commitment to combining strong legal knowledge with genuine care for every client he represents.

Mukesh believes every client deserves direct access to their advocate, not just a case number passed between staff. He works closely with each person from the first consultation through resolution, ensuring they understand their options and feel supported at every stage of their case.

Based in Karachi, Mukesh is dedicated to serving the local community through his practice. Outside of his work, he values time spent with family and remains committed to upholding the highest standards of legal representation for the people of Karachi.
