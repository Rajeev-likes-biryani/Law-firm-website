Kamlesh is an associate practicing in the areas of criminal defense and constitutional law. He was enrolled to practice before the courts of Pakistan in 2016, and has since built experience representing clients in criminal trials, bail applications, and constitutional petitions before the High Court of Sindh.

Kamlesh graduated from the Sindh Muslim Law College in 2015, where he was recognized for his work in the college's legal aid clinic. Prior to joining Mukesh Kumar Khatri & Co, he gained experience assisting on high-profile criminal defense matters in Karachi.

Kamlesh believes every client deserves a strong, dedicated defense regardless of the charges they face. He combines thorough case preparation with steady courtroom advocacy to protect his clients' rights at every stage of their case.

Kamlesh is based in Karachi, where he was born and raised. Outside of his practice, he enjoys playing cricket, following current affairs, and spending time with his family.
